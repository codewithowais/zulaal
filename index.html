Our Story

Water as it was
meant to be

Zulaal — meaning crystal-clear water in Arabic — was born from a simple belief: every Pakistani deserves water that is genuinely, verifiably pure. Not just labelled pure. Actually pure.

We built a brand that holds itself to the highest standard. Quarterly PCRWR testing. 7-stage reverse osmosis purification. A custom bottle service that turns hydration into a statement piece.
